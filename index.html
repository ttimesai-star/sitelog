The problem

On a building site the client's inspector writes defect remarks: props too far apart, honeycombing at a column, missing rebar spacers. That log usually sits in the contractor's office or in a database someone else runs. Before acceptance a line can be edited, lost or marked "closed", and nobody can prove what was written.

2

How SiteLog works

Remarks are written to Arkiv from the inspector's wallet as readonly entities. The page trusts a record only if its on-chain $creator is an inspector in the roster published by the client's wallet. A field anyone can type, like "author", is never used.

The live journal is read straight from the public Tiramisu RPC: there is no SiteLog server. The demo contractor tried to forge a "resolved" remark and to close its own defect. Both are on chain, and both are shown and ignored.
